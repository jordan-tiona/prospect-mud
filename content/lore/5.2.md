The Drask are scaled, plantigrade humanoids with a distinctly
lizardlike face and elongated snout, on a fully functional humanoid
body plan with hands. They are warm-blooded and self-regulating:
Drask do not bask, need no heated environments, and share ordinary
rooms and streets on the same terms as everyone else.

Build: dense and broad-chested, the heaviest of the mid-height
peoples, around 1.9 m and 95 to 110 kg on average.

Head: an elongated snout with the teeth hidden at rest; they show
only when the mouth is open. From the brow back over the skull and
down the neck the scales are larger and protrude, angled backward
like shingles, so a hand run from brow to neck slides over them and a
hand run the other way catches and pulls. The tongue is dark and
unforked. There are no external ears; hearing is through a covered
membrane flush with the side of the head.

Scales are fine and smooth on the face, hands and underside, larger
and keeled on the back, shoulders and tail, in ochre, slate-green,
rust and bone, banded rather than spotted. Drask shed in patches
rather than whole skins and treat it as grooming.

The tail is thick and muscular, long enough to touch the ground, and
carried clear of the floor when walking. It dictates how a Drask
sits: on stools, on the front edge of a chair, or on seats made with
a gap for it.

Hands and feet have four digits each, three fingers and an opposable
thumb on the hand. Claws are left untrimmed but short, a few
centimetres at most.

Drask wear ordinary shirts and upper garments. Below the waist they
wear a loincloth, not necessarily thin, which exists mainly to cover
and protect the genitals; trousers are not the norm.
