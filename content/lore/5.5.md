The Astrel were engineered for hazardous orbital construction,
spacecraft maintenance, and other reduced-gravity or extraterrestrial
industrial work. Their colloquial or historic occupational
designation is Void Walkers. Astrel today are free to pursue any
profession in societies recognizing their rights; their engineering
purpose is not their inevitable vocation.

They are the tallest people, 2.3 to 2.4 m and 70 to 80 kg on
average, with long limbs, narrow shoulders and a long neck, a frame
that looks stretched rather than thin. Their skin is smooth and
hairless, deep charcoal through ash grey, sometimes mottled, and it
dulls and mottles further with age. Their looks point to none of the
three ancestral peoples in particular.

Face: a short, blunt, hairless muzzle with a nose, lips and an
expressive mouth. Eyes are large, suited to dim work, with visible
whites and a transparent inner lid that closes against glare. Ears
are small and flat to the head.

Hands have four long fingers with an extra joint and one thumb. Feet
are prehensile, with a grasping big toe for anchoring in low gravity;
shoes fit badly, and bare or open-toed feet are the everyday norm.
They have no tail.

At full gravity they tire faster over a long shift and favour sitting
or leaning, so Astrel are often found perched on rails and ledges.
They have enhanced radiation resilience, which does not let them
survive vacuum; suits and life support remain necessary.

Astrel dress in close-fitting layered garments with attachment loops
and many pockets, a habit carried over from generations of suit
liners and now worn by choice.
