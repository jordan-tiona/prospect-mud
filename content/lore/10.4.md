The combined ecological and infrastructural disasters eventually
rendered Haven unable to sustain its population.

Some inhabitants escaped to the orbital habitats. Many others
perished or were trapped in isolated districts.

The details of the final evacuation, population losses, government
response, and possible surviving enclaves remain unresolved.

The city was abandoned as a permanent civilian settlement.
