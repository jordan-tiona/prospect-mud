Approximately 150 years before the present, Haven suffered a
catastrophic incursion of indigenous Prospect life into its closed
environmental systems.

The event became known as the Bloom.

For centuries, the city had relied on carefully maintained barriers
separating its internal ecology from the planetary biosphere.

But the city's residents continually interacted with Prospect through
resource extraction, trade, scientific research, and surface
expeditions.

Small contamination events were inevitable.

Eventually, an organism, collection of organisms, or ecological
process entered the city and established itself in an environment
where Haven's defenses could not effectively contain it.

The precise biology and initial contamination pathway have not been
determined.
