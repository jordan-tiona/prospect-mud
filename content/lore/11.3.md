Freehold began as an orbital construction and manufacturing yard, built
and owned by Haven-based interests and staffed substantially by
engineered peoples. After a long labour and political struggle, its
workforce gained ownership of the yard and declared it an independent
habitat. The name commemorates freedom from the historic treatment of
engineered peoples as property. Freehold's independence came before the
Bloom.

Freehold is owned collectively by the people who live and work there.
Its industrial plant is held in common and run through work
cooperatives, which also form the basis of its government: a
Freeholder's standing comes from their cooperative and their trade, and
there is no private ownership of the habitat's heavy industry.

Freehold is the Constellation's heavy-industry habitat: shipbuilding,
ship repair, pressure equipment and structural fabrication. Its yards
have built or extended much of the Constellation's large structure,
including later sections of Elsewhere's cylinder. Physically it is a
working yard first and a home second: a long spine of docks and
fabrication halls with spun habitation rings grown along it over the
centuries, plain and well kept.

All seven peoples live on Freehold, but the Astrel, Voldren and Selk are
the majority, and its culture is theirs by weight of numbers: plain
speech, pride in skilled work, impatience with rank that isn't earned,
and a settled conviction that collective ownership is simply the correct
way to run a habitat. Elsewhere and Freehold are on good terms, and have
bought each other's work for as long as both have existed. Elsewherians
generally regard Freeholders as blunt, dependable and a little preachy
about ownership; Freeholders regard Elsewhere as comfortable and
talkative.
