Elsewhere was the first major independent orbital habitat, founded by
people who rejected being governed by Haven, not necessarily
government as such. It is approximately 400 years old in the game's
present; this remains an approximate worldbuilding date rather than a
fixed calendar entry. It existed roughly two and a half centuries
before Haven's Bloom, using the accepted approximate chronology.

Elsewhere is a rotating cylinder with one immense open inhabited
interior. The current working measurements are 30 km long by 2 km in
diameter. Most ordinary residential landscapes lie along the inner
cylindrical surface, where rotation supplies artificial gravity
(approximately Earth-normal at the rim is a working assumption).
Standing in its streets, inhabitants can look across or upward toward
the landscape curving overhead. A massive artificial light source
runs lengthwise along the central axis. Its illumination technology,
day/night behavior and power systems need not be specified for the
prototype.

A largely stationary docking complex at one end handles arrivals and
departures, with a rotating/stationary transfer interface and radial
elevators connecting the central axis to the inhabited rim. Their
engineering details remain undecided. Local streets and some form of
lengthwise mass transit are logical possibilities, but specific
transit designs, stop inventories and operating systems are not
fixed.
