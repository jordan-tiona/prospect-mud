Lantern was founded before the Bloom by scholars, institutes and their
households leaving Haven for the freedom to pursue inquiry Haven
restricted. It is governed under a research charter by its institutes.

Lantern is the Constellation's centre of learning and research:
Venn–Saye dynamics, Prospect's biology (studied on samples brought up
from the surface), life-support science and instruments. It is also the
one habitat that permits carefully controlled research into machine
autonomy, under oversight it considers strict and the rest of the
Constellation considers inadequate. This is the single most common thing
said about Lantern elsewhere. Everywhere else the old prohibitions
stand; no unrestricted machine intelligence exists anywhere in the
Constellation, and Lantern's research has produced none.

Elsewherians think of Lantern as clever, strange and a little dangerous.
They admire its learning, send gifted children there to study, and say
some version of "Lantern will get us all killed one day" without quite
believing it. Lantern thinks of Elsewhere as sensible and incurious.
Fine instruments, advanced Fieldworks equipment and specialist training
are the things to go to Lantern for.
