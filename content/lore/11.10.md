Tailings is not one structure but a cluster of old ore-processing and
refining platforms, built before the Bloom to process material lifted
from Prospect's surface, that became a settlement because the people who
worked on them stayed. It was never chartered as a habitat and has no
single government: each platform is run by whoever runs it, and what
coordination exists is informal. The others count it as a habitat
because it is too large and too permanent to count as anything else.

Living there is cheap and hard. Work is available to anyone who can do
it, and few questions are asked about who someone was before.
Elsewhere's regulation and Meridian's duties reach it unevenly, and
goods and people that want to avoid either tend to pass through
Tailings.

Elsewherians speak of Tailings with a mixture of pity and suspicion. A
person "ended up on Tailings", and a thing of doubtful origin "came
through Tailings". Raw material, scrap, machine parts of uncertain
provenance and labour are what it has to offer, and it is the obvious
place in the Constellation for a person who does not want to be found.
