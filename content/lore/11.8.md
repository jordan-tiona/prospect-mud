Anchorage was Haven's orbital port: the stationary station where traffic
between the city on the surface and the habitats changed ships. It was
built, owned and run by Haven, and before the Bloom it was Haven's
presence in orbit rather than a habitat in its own right.

When Haven fell, Anchorage took in more of the evacuees than any other
single place, and then closed itself to the surface for good: nothing
from Prospect has docked there since. Its holds, docks and transit halls
were converted to housing in the emergency and never converted back, and
it has been crowded ever since. Its people are largely the descendants
of Haven's survivors.

Anchorage holds Haven's surviving records and archives, and keeps Haven's
forms, manners and public ceremonies alive with a formality the other
habitats find old-fashioned. Its government maintains that it is the
lawful continuation of Haven's government. No other habitat recognises
the claim and most ignore it, but it has never been withdrawn, and it is
the main reason the question of Haven's succession is still a live one
150 years on.

Anchorage is poorer than it was, proud, and inclined to believe the
habitats abandoned Haven. An Elsewherian thinks of Anchorage as formal,
crowded and living in the past, and of its people as decent and a little
sad; Anchorage thinks of Elsewhere as the habitat that left first. If a
family wants to know who its people were in Haven, Anchorage is where
the records are.
