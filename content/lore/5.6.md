The Voldren were engineered for mining, excavation and other
dangerous work underground. Cave Crawlers is an occupational or
colloquial designation, potentially harsher or more derogatory in
some circumstances than the other two engineered peoples' labels.
Precisely who uses it neutrally, disparagingly or as a reclaimed term
is unresolved. Modern Voldren need not be miners.

They are the shortest people, 1.3 to 1.4 m and 90 to 110 kg on
average, as heavy as a Drask at two thirds the height, with broad
shoulders, a thick neck, short limbs, big hands and a low centre of
gravity that makes a Voldren very hard to knock over. Their looks
point to none of the three ancestral peoples in particular.

Skin is thick, leathery, textured and hairless, with harder keratin
plating over the forearms, shoulders, shins and the backs of the
hands, in rock colours: umber, red-brown, dark olive and basalt grey.
With age it lines and cracks like dried mud rather than wrinkling.

Face: a heavy brow ridge and a short, broad muzzle, teeth hidden at
rest, with wide nostrils lined with dense bristles that filter dust.
Eyes are small and deep-set under the brow, with large pupils for low
light; bright light makes them squint, so Voldren on Elsewhere
habitually wear a brimmed cap or visor under the axial light. Ears
are small and recessed under a flap of skin that keeps grit out.

Hands have four thick fingers and a thumb with heavy hoof-like nails
rather than claws, the strongest grip of the seven peoples and the
least fine control. Feet are plantigrade, broad and thick-soled; even
so, the conditions they were made for punished bare feet and hands,
so Voldren have always worked in boots and gloves. Gloves are rarer
now; the preference for a good pair of boots remains. They have no
tail.

They resist heat, contaminants and abrasion, but are not
invulnerable. Voldren dress in sturdy layered clothing with
reinforced knees and elbows, worn by habit even by those who have
never been underground, with good boots and the cap or visor.

Males and females differ little beyond reproductive anatomy and
voice; females' brow ridge is lower. A Voldren lives around 95 years,
is adult by about 18 and elderly from about 75.
