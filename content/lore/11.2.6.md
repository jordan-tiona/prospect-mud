Elsewhere has a four-century independent history, mixed-species civic
equality, pride in self-rule, a complex mature economy, and visibly
distinct old and newer regions. Its inhabitants can be simultaneously
proud Elsewherians and strongly attached to a species or ancestral
cultural heritage; neither identity cancels the other.

Plausible NPC texture, not universal traits: different residents may
interpret the charter differently, respect practical maintenance
expertise, argue over how much government should provide, debate the
treatment of older neighborhoods, or experience varying degrees of
attachment to the founding story. A lifelong Old Quarter resident and
a person raised near the agricultural Reach may have different
experiences and interests without belonging to fixed personality
types. The Bloom is approximately 150 years in the past, encountered
by most ordinary contemporaries through education, inherited stories,
historical debate or institutions rather than firsthand experience.

Specific holidays, dialects, styles of dress, prominent parties,
religious customs, named civic offices and neighborhood stereotypes
are not decided.
