The Constellation has seven major habitats, with smaller stations
scattered between them. Besides Elsewhere there are Freehold, the
shipyards; Meridian, the markets; Anchorage, which was Haven's port;
Lantern, the scholars' habitat; Tailings, the old processing platforms;
and Hermitage, which keeps to itself. Each habitat governs itself and
answers to no one else. People go between habitats by ferry. Most
Elsewherians have visited one or two and know only what everyone says
about the rest.
