The Lirra are avian-influenced, feathered humanoids with functional
hands. They are flightless and have no wings; long flight-feather
fringes run along the forearms and, shorter, along the upper arms,
vestigial and decorative.

Build: the lightest of the mid-height peoples, around 1.9 m and 50 to
55 kg on average, light-boned.

Face: a short, blunt, beak-like muzzle with a hard edge where lips
would be and teeth behind it; the face is feathered and keeps enough
movement for expression. Eyes are raptor-like, filling the socket so
little or no white shows, with a pale, intense iris under a bony brow
shelf, and set more to the sides than a Kell's, so a Lirra turns the
head to look straight at someone. There are no external ears; the ear
opening sits behind the eye under a small fan of short feathers. A
crest of long feathers runs from the brow back over the head, raised
and flattened with mood.

True feathers cover the head, neck, arms and back, with short down on
the body; they moult, which shows age and health. The tail is long
and thin, feathered flat along its length with a short plume at the
tip.

Legs are digitigrade, with scaled, properly muscular lower legs,
three forward toes and a rear toe, and bare feet. Hands are scaled
and bare, with four long fingers and a thumb, the finest grip of the
seven peoples.

Each Lirra's plumage uses a small palette, two colours at most, with
the crest usually the brighter one.

Lirra wear loose, draped garments, wrapped and pinned, that cover the
body fully while leaving the arms free so the fringes are not
crushed.
