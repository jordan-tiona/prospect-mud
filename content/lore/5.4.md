The Revari are a long-established, self-reproducing people with
ancestry involving the Kell, Drask and Lirra. They are a recognizable
people with broad inherited variation, not arbitrary assemblages of
body parts, and their name is their own identity, not a translation
of "hybrid".

Their base is the Kell silhouette: rangy, digitigrade, furred,
mid-height, with a full tail, around 2 m and 65 to 70 kg on average.
An individual shows at most two or three visible non-Kell traits,
drawn only from a fixed set: patches of fine scales on the hands,
forearms, shins, brow, shoulder blades, abdomen or hips; a feather
fringe on the forearms, a feather crest on the crown in place of the
ruff, or feathers mixed through the ruff itself; a scaled Drask-like
tail, Kell-sized or heavier and often furred at the tip, or a thin
feathered Lirra-like one; Drask-style covered ear
membranes in place of Kell ears; a shorter, broader muzzle. Everything
else is Kell. Traits run in families rather than appearing at random;
a Revari's parents and siblings share most of their visible traits.
No Revari has a full Drask snout, a beak, true feathers over the
whole body, or Lirra legs and feet.

Eyes are Kell eyes by default, including the reflective layer; a pale
Lirra-type iris is a possible inherited trait. Colouring follows the
Kell palette, with scale and feather patches following Drask and
Lirra colouring where they appear.

Dress is mixed by family: Revari with a ruff dress as the Kell do,
and the rest wear ordinary clothes.

As with the Kell, males carry the longer ruff and females the longer
tail crest, and male voices are lower. Irises come from the Kell set,
amber, gold, copper, pale green or grey, or the pale Lirra-type iris,
with a green-gold eyeshine in dim light. A Revari lives around 90
years, is adult by about 18 and elderly from about 70.
