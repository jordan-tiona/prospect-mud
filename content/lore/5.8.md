All seven peoples must be capable of broadly shared habitation and
ordinary community life. Species may affect appearance, anatomy,
environmental tolerance or modest gameplay capabilities, but must not
determine intelligence, morality, personality, politics, class, or
profession. Citizenship, upbringing, culture, economic position and
personal experience are separate character dimensions.

The engineered peoples' formal names (Otheryn, Voldren, Neshyr) and
their descriptive historical labels (Void Walkers, Cave Crawlers, Sea
Walkers) can coexist. Historical exploitation makes context and
speaker important; the setting has not settled their uniform social
meaning. The naming set as a whole is accepted for development, but
remains revisable.
