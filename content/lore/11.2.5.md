Elsewhere is a representative, civic-minded charter republic that
values both individual liberty and substantial public institutions.
Its founders sought local self-government and political freedom from
Haven rather than an absence of government. Its foundational civic
principles are:

1. Equal personhood and citizenship: Equal fundamental rights for all
   seven peoples, whether ancestral, hybrid-descended or engineered;
   this is a founding commitment, not a later concession.
2. Self-government: Legitimate authority arises from Elsewhere's
   residents, not an external planetary government.
3. Individual liberty: Strong protection against arbitrary rule,
   coercion and involuntary servitude.
4. Civic responsibility: Public institutions and collective
   investment are legitimate and necessary to sustain shared life,
   particularly on an enclosed orbital habitat.

Representative lawmaking, executive administration and an independent
judiciary are established broad structural directions.

This commitment to equality is compatible with individual prejudice,
disagreements over public spending and regulation, material
inequality, and friction between historic and newer areas. Elsewhere
is not a politically homogeneous utopia. The exact point at which
Haven abolished the older systems of coerced labor remains unknown;
Elsewhere's founding principle does not settle it.
