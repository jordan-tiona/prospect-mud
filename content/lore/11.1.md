The Constellation consists of independent orbital habitats that
developed over centuries while Haven still existed.

Their origins are not primarily a response to the Bloom.

Instead, habitats were constructed by populations seeking
independence from Haven's politics, laws, social institutions,
economic structures, and cultural expectations.

Other habitats arose from commercial ventures, industrial facilities,
political movements, and specialized orbital infrastructure.

As the habitats became economically self-sufficient, they developed
distinct governments and social identities.
