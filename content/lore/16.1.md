The home civilization possessed advanced spaceflight, orbital
settlement, biotechnology, resource extraction, and sophisticated
automation. Interstellar travel required generation ships sustaining
populations across centuries; routine interstellar transportation
was unavailable to that civilization.

The ancient AI crisis led to restrictions on general-purpose
autonomous machine intelligences and independent, open-ended agency.
Computers, expert systems, robotics, and automated industry remained
widespread. Haven's failed containment network need not have been an
unrestricted general-purpose AI.

Engineered biological capabilities are bounded. Astrel (Void
Walkers) require pressure suits and life support; Selk (Sea
Walkers) breathe air and can live in dry shared accommodations; no
engineered people is universally immune to Prospect. Technology
should remain sophisticated but fallible. Venn-Saye dynamics adds one
invented field and internally constrained magic-like effects.
Ordinary Fieldworks tools are technologically operated and require no
genetic adaptation; Dynamicists require both a school-specific
genetic alteration and a catalyst.
