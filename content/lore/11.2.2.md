Elsewhere was initially a much shorter cylinder, not simply a cylinder
of smaller diameter. Over approximately four centuries, it was
extended along its longitudinal axis away from the original dockside
end. The older sections near the docks are therefore denser, more
heavily rebuilt and more urban. As one travels toward the opposite
end, development generally becomes newer, less crowded and more
spacious; the farthest section is substantially agricultural and
rural. These are trends, not hard boundaries or a rigid zoning law.
Historic expansion seams and repurposed structures are plausible
visual details, not prescribed engineering.

This longitudinal historical gradient is to Elsewhere what the
layered vertical history is to Haven. The age of a neighborhood can be
inferred from its place along the cylinder, with local exceptions from
renovations and redevelopment.
