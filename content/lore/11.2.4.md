Elsewhere is a mature, highly diversified orbital society, built up
over four centuries. It provides broad everyday commerce, technical
services, manufacturing, repair, education, medicine, food
production, entertainment and Venn-Saye-related services. It remains
integrated into inter-habitat trade and the extraction economy of
Prospect.

Elsewhere is an ideal new-player hub, where characters can find
nearly any basic goods, training, services, profession entry point or
equipment category. More distinctive specialized or rare goods and
opportunities can motivate visits to other habitats. Elsewhere must
still feel like a complete, inhabited society and remain meaningful to
advanced players, not a tutorial-only staging area.
