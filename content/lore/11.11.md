Hermitage was founded before the Bloom by a movement that left Haven to
live apart under a shared discipline of restraint and self-sufficiency.
Outsiders do not agree on whether it is a religion, a philosophy or
simply a way of life. Hermitage's people keep to it, do not proselytise,
and do not discuss it much with outsiders.

At the Bloom, Hermitage sealed its docks against contamination and kept
them sealed for about a century, long after the other habitats had
satisfied themselves of the danger. It reopened within living memory,
roughly two generations ago, and has traded cautiously since. A century
alone made it self-sufficient to a degree no other habitat matches:
Hermitage makes, grows and mends nearly everything it uses, and its
cultivars, textiles and handwork are distinct enough to be recognised
anywhere.

Its population is small, and its people are quiet, plain in dress and
manner, and noticed when they travel because they so rarely do.
Elsewherians find Hermitage admirable, odd and slightly unnerving in
about equal measure, and say "nobody really knows what they get up to",
which is true. Hermitage, for its part, thinks Elsewhere is loud.
