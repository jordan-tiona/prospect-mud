By the time Haven fell, the Constellation had already existed for
centuries.

It possessed established populations, economies, and political
institutions.

The habitats provided refuge for survivors, though the willingness
and ability to accept evacuees may have differed.

Rebuilding Haven would have required enormous resources, exposed
populations to unresolved biological hazards, and potentially
restored the authority of a former political center.

The habitats ultimately chose not to rebuild it.

The precise decision-making process has not been established. There
may never have been a single unified vote.

Over the subsequent 150 years, the Constellation became the sole
enduring center of civilization in the system, while the surface of
Prospect continued serving industrial, scientific, and exploratory
purposes.
