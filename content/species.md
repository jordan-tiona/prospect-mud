# The seven peoples

<!-- Optional shared preface; drop it if the picker only has room for per-entry text. -->

All seven peoples came to Prospect together, aboard the nine generation ships that left their shared home system long ago. Three are ancestral peoples, one descends from all three, and three were engineered in the home system for dangerous work. On Elsewhere, every one of them holds the same citizenship.

## Kethari

Furred, canine-looking people who walk on the balls of their feet, with mobile, expressive ears and a wide range of coats and builds. One of the three ancestral peoples of the home system. Two Kethari scientists were the first to discover the Venn–Saye field, and many Kethari communities still hold that scientific tradition as a point of cultural pride.

<!-- Left out as undecided in bible 5.1: tail length, muzzle proportions. The proposed starting-proficiency bonus is a mechanics question for the owner and Dev, not something this entry promises. -->

## Sylari

Scaled people with a distinctly lizardlike face and elongated snout, on an otherwise upright body plan with fully capable hands. One of the three ancestral peoples of the home system. They need no special warmth or conditions, and share the same streets and rooms as everyone else.

<!-- Left out as unfinished in bible 5.2: cranial features, scale patterns, the tail. The third sentence rests on 5.2's design boundary (no Earth-reptile physiology or heated environments) and 5.8's shared-habitation rule, not on a settled metabolism. -->

## Aevyrin

Feathered, avian-influenced people, lighter in frame than the other ancestral peoples, with expressive plumage or crests. One of the three ancestral peoples of the home system.

<!-- Left out as unresolved in bible 5.3: beak or birdlike facial anatomy, any flight-related structures, flightlessness. "Lighter in frame" paraphrases 5.3's "distinctive balance, skeletal proportions"; cut it if that reads as more than the bible says. -->

## Naevari

A long-established people whose ancestry draws on the Kethari, Sylari and Aevyrin alike. Most resemble the furred Kethari in general build, but individuals carry varying inherited traits from all three lines, so no two families look quite the same. They are a people in their own right, not a mixture, and their name is their own.

<!-- Left out as visual proposals in bible 5.4: localized scales, feathered crests, differing ear or tail shapes. The origin of their cross-ancestry is unresolved and not mentioned. -->

## Otheryn

Engineered in the home system for hazardous work in orbit and low gravity, such as construction and spacecraft maintenance, which earned them the old label "Void Walkers". Like the other engineered peoples, their ancestors were once legally property; on Elsewhere they are citizens like anyone else and take up whatever work they choose.

<!-- Left out as Provisional in bible 5.5: tall or lanky build, long limbs, little or no hair, radiation resilience, possible discomfort in high gravity. If the owner wants one physical sentence here, "tall and long-limbed" is the least committal of those. -->

## Voldren

Engineered in the home system for mining, excavation and other dangerous work underground. The old label "Cave Crawlers" still follows them, and whether it is neutral or an insult depends very much on who is saying it. Modern Voldren work at whatever they choose, miners or not, and on Elsewhere hold the same citizenship as everyone else.

<!-- Left out as Provisional in bible 5.6: short, broad, powerful build, thick or leathery skin, recessed ears, hazard resistance. The label's society-wide meaning is unresolved (5.6, 5.8), which is why the entry says "depends on who is saying it" rather than settling it. -->

## Neshyr

Engineered in the home system for hazardous underwater work, such as deep construction, extraction and salvage, which earned them the old label "Sea Walkers". They breathe air and live in ordinary dry quarters alongside everyone else; the water was their work, never their home. Like the other engineered peoples, they are full citizens on Elsewhere and follow any trade they like.
