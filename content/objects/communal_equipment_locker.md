# a communal equipment locker

## Short Description

A communal equipment locker stands against one wall, its door unlatched.

## Description

A tall, plain locker shared by the people who live around the courtyard. The door is left unlatched so anyone can open it. Inside, gardening kits hang from a row of hooks, more of them than anyone is likely to need at once.

## Probe Description

The locker has been repainted more than once, in shades that don't quite match, like the walls around it. The hinges are oiled and the door swings easily. Taped inside the door is a handwritten note asking people to take what they need and bring it back when they're done.
