## Glance

The smell of something freshly brewed meets you at the door. Cups clink behind a low counter, and the conversation around a handful of occupied tables settles into a comfortable, uneven murmur.

## Description

The Corner Café is small enough that the counter is visible from almost every seat. The tables don't quite match, and the edges of a few chairs have been rubbed smooth by frequent use. Cups and neatly stacked dishes occupy shelves behind the counter.

A window looks out onto Old Quarter Square. People at the tables talk over drinks, read, or simply watch the movement outside. There's a little noise from the equipment behind the counter, but it rarely rises above the conversation.

The room feels more useful than fashionable: somewhere to sit for a few minutes, meet a neighbor, or stay longer than intended. Mara Voss runs the place.
