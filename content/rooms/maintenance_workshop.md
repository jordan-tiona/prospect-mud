## Glance

A small motor whines on a test bench, rising briefly above the clink of metal tools. The air smells faintly of warm machinery, and most of the available work surfaces are occupied by equipment awaiting attention.

## Description

Workbenches line the walls, crowded with tools, tagged components, and pieces of equipment in various stages of repair. A few cabinets stand open, their shelves more orderly than the benches. Old scuffs and newer patches mark the floor near the heavier workstations.

The workshop serves the aging infrastructure of the Old Quarter. Nothing here is arranged for visitors, although a clear strip of floor leaves room to move between the benches. A steady mechanical hum comes from a test unit, interrupted now and then by a brief click or rattle.

Tarin Os works here. Most of what fills the room would make immediate sense to him; to anyone else, it takes a closer look to tell which parts belong together.
