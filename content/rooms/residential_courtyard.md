## Glance

The noise of the square falls away in a courtyard enclosed by apartment walls. Leaves stir above a narrow path, and a faint exchange of voices drifts down from the surrounding windows.

## Description

The courtyard is a shared space between closely built residential blocks. A narrow path crosses worn paving, passing low planting beds and a few seats tucked against the walls. Some windows stand open; others are shaded or crowded with small household things.

Here, the Old Quarter feels less hurried. The sounds of the square are still present, but softened by the surrounding buildings. Every so often a door opens onto the courtyard, or voices carry briefly from an upper level before fading again.

Repairs and alterations have left the walls uneven in color and finish. None of it seems arranged to impress visitors. It's simply a place where people live, with a little room between their doors.
