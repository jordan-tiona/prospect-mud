## Glance

A low mechanical hum fills the concourse as passengers emerge from the lifts. Footsteps and rolling cases echo off the hard floor, mingling with voices calling greetings or working out where to go next.

## Description

The concourse is a broad, practical space at the foot of the lifts that connect Elsewhere's inhabited surface to the central axis. Its floor is scuffed by years of traffic, and brighter replacement panels interrupt the older wall coverings. A steady hum comes from machinery behind the lift doors.

Arrivals pause to check their belongings or consult a directory, while others pass through without slowing. The air carries a faint smell of warm machinery beneath the ordinary scents of a busy public passage.

An open passage leads toward Old Quarter Square, where the tighter streets and older buildings begin. This is a place people pass through on their way somewhere else, though there's almost always someone waiting.
