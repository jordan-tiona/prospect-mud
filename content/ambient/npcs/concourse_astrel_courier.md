# An Astrel courier

## Poses

- checking the labels on a stack of delivery containers
- comparing a handheld display with the containers beside them
- adjusting a worn carrying strap across their shoulder

## Emotes

- The Astrel courier frowns at a line on the handheld and checks the nearest container's tag again.
- The Astrel courier shifts the carrying strap from one shoulder to the other.
- The Astrel courier leans a hip against a container and works a thumb down the handheld's list.
- The Astrel courier crosses out a routing tag and writes over it by hand.
- The Astrel courier spreads their long toes on the floor and rolls their shoulders, the way a long shift at full gravity asks for.

## Description

A tall, long-limbed Astrel courier stoops over a stack of delivery containers, a worn carrying strap crossing their close-fitting work jacket, a few scuffs marking the containers beside them. They compare the contents of a handheld display with the labels on the containers, occasionally adjusting the strap on their shoulder. For the moment, their attention is firmly on the delivery.

## Probe Description

The courier's jacket is a working one, close-fitting and patched at the elbows, hung with attachment loops and pockets, with a pale square on the chest where some badge or patch used to be. Their skin is ash grey, mottled a little darker at the temples, and their long-jointed fingers work down the handheld's list a line at a time. The containers are the standard stacking kind, each with a routing tag, and a couple of the tags have been crossed out and rewritten by hand. The handheld display shows a list, and the courier works down it with a thumb, now and then frowning at a line and glancing at the stack before moving on. Between stacks they lean a hip against the nearest container, the way Astrel do when a shift at full gravity has gone on long enough; their feet are bare, the long toes spread on the concourse floor. They have the look of someone who has done this a thousand times and would like to be done with it by the end of the shift.
