# A Selk neighbor

## Poses

- checking the soil around a row of small plants
- gathering a few fallen leaves from the path
- tending one of the courtyard's planting beds

## Emotes

- The Selk neighbor pulls a weed, shakes the soil from its roots and drops it into the cloth bag.
- The Selk neighbor straightens a labelled stake that has leaned over.
- The Selk neighbor glances up at a sound from one of the homes above, then goes back to the plants.
- The Selk neighbor presses the soil firm around a stem with the back of the hand fork.
- The Selk neighbor sits back on their heels and brushes the soil from their knees.

## Description

A Selk resident tends the plants beside the courtyard path, pausing now and then to clear away a leaf or inspect a stem. Their sleeveless everyday clothes are smudged at the knees from working near the soil, and the dark of their back shades to pale at the throat. They seem comfortable making an unhurried job of it.

## Probe Description

The neighbor works along the row with a small hand fork and a cloth bag for whatever they pull up, one plant at a time. These beds are better kept than the square's: the soil is dark and loose, the edges tidy, a few stakes labelled in a neat hand. Their arms are bare, the fins along the forearms lying flat as they work, and their everyday clothes have clearly been worn for this before, the knees permanently darkened. The broad, flat Selk shoes beside the bed are caked at the edges with soil. When a door opens somewhere above, they glance up, nod to whoever it is, and go back to the stem they were checking.
