# A Selk neighbor

## Poses

- checking the soil around a row of small plants
- gathering a few fallen leaves from the path
- tending one of the courtyard's planting beds

## Description

A Selk resident tends the plants beside the courtyard path, pausing now and then to clear away a leaf or inspect a stem. Their everyday clothes are smudged at the knees from working near the soil. They seem comfortable making an unhurried job of it.

## Probe Description

The neighbor works along the row with a small hand fork and a cloth bag for whatever they pull up, one plant at a time. These beds are better kept than the square's: the soil is dark and loose, the edges tidy, a few stakes labelled in a neat hand. Their sleeves are pushed past the elbow, and their everyday clothes have clearly been worn for this before, the knees permanently darkened. When a door opens somewhere above, they glance up, nod to whoever it is, and go back to the stem they were checking.
