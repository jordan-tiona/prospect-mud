# An Otheryn courier

## Poses

- checking the labels on a stack of delivery containers
- comparing a handheld display with the containers beside them
- adjusting a worn carrying strap across their shoulder

## Description

A worn carrying strap crosses the courier's work jacket, and a few scuffs mark the stacked containers beside them. They compare the contents of a handheld display with the labels on the containers, occasionally adjusting the strap on their shoulder. For the moment, their attention is firmly on the delivery.

## Probe Description

The courier's jacket is a working one, patched at the elbows, with a pale square on the chest where some badge or patch used to be. The containers are the standard stacking kind, each with a routing tag, and a couple of the tags have been crossed out and rewritten by hand. The handheld display shows a list, and the courier works down it with a thumb, now and then frowning at a line and glancing at the stack before moving on. They have the look of someone who has done this a thousand times and would like to be done with it by the end of the shift.
