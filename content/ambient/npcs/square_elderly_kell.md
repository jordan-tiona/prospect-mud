# An elderly Kell

## Poses

- reading quietly from a tablet resting in their lap
- glancing up from their tablet at people crossing the square
- turning an ear toward the sounds of the square

## Description

Gray has crept into the fur around the Kell's muzzle, and the long ruff down their back has gone pale and thin. A slim reading tablet rests in their lap, held at an easy angle, while their big leathery ears occasionally turn toward the sounds of the square. They seem content to read and let the bustle carry on around them.

## Probe Description

The tablet in their lap is an old one, its casing scuffed pale at the corners, and the text on it is set large. They read slowly and without hurry, one ear swivelling toward any raised voice or rattling cart while the other stays trained on the page. A walking stick leans against the bench beside them, its handle worn to a shine. Their coat, cream dappled with slate, is well brushed, and the front-panel they wear is neat and a little old-fashioned, its shoulder strips tied with care; their feet are wrapped in soft, much-washed cloth, and their tail lies along the bench. The bench they have chosen is the one with the best view of the whole square.
