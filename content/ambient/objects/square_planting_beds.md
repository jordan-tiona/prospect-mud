# Communal planting beds

## Short Description

Low planting beds filled with shrubs and flowers border the square's paving.

## Description

Leaves and small flowers spill over the low edges of the beds, softening the square's worn paving. The soil is dark and carefully tended, though a few bare patches remain between the plants. Narrow irrigation fittings are visible beneath the foliage, almost hidden until you look closely.
