# Public notice board

## Short Description

A glass-fronted notice board hangs beside one of the passages leading into the square.

## Description

Community notices and small advertisements are arranged behind a scratched transparent cover. Some are neatly printed; others have been written by hand. The frame has been repainted more than once, and the edges of older postings show beneath the newer ones. Nothing on the board suggests that the square's residents lack things to discuss.

## Probe Description

Behind the cover, the postings are layered three or four deep in places. The printed ones tend to be practical, a repair offered, a neighborhood meeting, a room's worth of furniture to give away, and the handwritten ones run more personal: something found, something lost, a short note of thanks to a neighbor. Dates on the older layers go back a long way, and nobody seems to have had the heart to strip them. The cover's latch is worn bright from use, and someone has wedged a folded scrap of paper into the frame where it no longer closes flush.
