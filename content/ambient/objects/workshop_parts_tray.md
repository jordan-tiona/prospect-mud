# Parts tray

## Short Description

A shallow tray of labeled spare parts rests on the edge of a workbench.

## Description

Shallow compartments hold small fasteners, tagged metal fittings, and a few sealed electronic modules. The labels are compact and practical, written for someone who already knows the equipment. A clear space beside the tray suggests the pieces are being sorted for a job in progress.

## Probe Description

The compartments are arranged by what the parts do rather than what they look like: fasteners of a few sizes in one row, fittings tagged with the systems they belong to in the next, and the sealed modules last, each with a short printed code and a date. Several labels have been written over more than once. Two compartments are empty with their labels still in place, and a third holds a single part set apart from the rest by a strip of tape across it, marked as not to be used. The clear space beside the tray carries a faint outline of grime where something larger was set down and taken away again.
