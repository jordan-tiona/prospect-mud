# Worn benches

## Short Description

Several weathered benches stand around the square's planted beds.

## Description

The benches are sturdy but show their age. Their seats have been polished smooth by use, while scuffs and small dents mark the edges. One has newer supports that don't quite match the rest of its frame. They're placed where someone could rest, eat, or simply watch people pass through the square.
