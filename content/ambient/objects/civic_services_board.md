# Public services board

## Short Description

A public information board beside the counter carries notices about neighborhood services.

## Description

Neatly arranged notices explain where residents can bring general inquiries, public-service questions, and maintenance concerns. Among the practical information is a brief reminder of Elsewhere's founding commitment to equal citizenship for all seven peoples. The board is kept tidy, though one corner is crowded with overlapping notices.

## Probe Description

Up close, the notices sort themselves into groups: where to bring a general inquiry, which part of the counter handles public-service questions, how to report something broken in a shared passage. Most are printed on the same plain stock and dated in one corner, and a few of the older ones have been corrected by hand where a detail changed. The reminder of Elsewhere's founding commitment to equal citizenship for all seven peoples sits near the middle with a little clear space around it, as if whoever keeps the board wants it left unobstructed. The crowded corner is short-term notices, a meeting, a temporary closure, a lost item, pinned over one another faster than anyone has taken the old ones down.
