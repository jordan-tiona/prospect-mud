# Public services board

## Short Description

A public information board beside the counter carries notices about neighborhood services.

## Description

Neatly arranged notices explain where residents can bring general inquiries, public-service questions, and maintenance concerns. Among the practical information is a brief reminder of Elsewhere's founding commitment to equal citizenship for all seven peoples. The board is kept tidy, though one corner is crowded with overlapping notices.
